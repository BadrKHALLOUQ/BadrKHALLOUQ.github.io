Selected Geospatial Research

Three maps illustrating the core of my spatial research

A curated selection from a broader cartographic production developed through doctoral and applied research. These maps illustrate three complementary dimensions of territorial change in the Sidi Kacem region: urban expansion, collective-land vulnerability and agricultural land valorization potential.

The portfolio intentionally presents only three representative maps rather than the full cartographic corpus, allowing each visual to communicate a distinct research question, analytical method and territorial implication.
Urban Dynamics
Selected Map · 01

Urban Expansion and Projected Growth · 1985–2050

Multi-temporal mapping of built-up expansion across the study area, combining observed urban development in 1985, 2005 and 2022 with projected built-up growth for 2040 and 2050.

The map highlights the spatial concentration of urbanization around existing settlements and major transport corridors, providing the spatial basis for assessing future pressure on agricultural and collective lands.

Research dimension Land-use change · Urban growth modelling · Spatial projection · Territorial dynamics

Land Vulnerability
Selected Map · 02

Prospective Vulnerability of Collective Lands to Urbanization · 2050

Prospective spatial assessment of collective-land exposure to urbanization by 2050. Vulnerability levels distinguish areas of very low, low, moderate and high prospective pressure.

The analysis identifies spatial hotspots where projected urban expansion may increasingly interact with collective farmland, supporting anticipatory land governance and territorial planning.

Research dimension Collective lands · Prospective vulnerability · Urbanization pressure · Land governance

GIS-AHP
Selected Map · 03

Agricultural Land Valorization Potential in Collective Farmlands

Spatial representation of agricultural land valorization potential within collective farmlands of the Sidi Kacem irrigated perimeter, classified into low, medium and high potential.

The final index integrates irrigation accessibility, existing crops, soil characteristics, road accessibility and slope through a GIS-based multi-criteria framework supported by the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP).

Research dimension GIS-AHP · Multi-criteria decision analysis · Agricultural land · Spatial decision support
